    How to play

    Climbing Grammar Castle

    • Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, or tap where you want to go.
    • Doors lead to other rooms. Every room you open appears on the map for the whole class.
    • Walk into a monster to battle. Each correct answer is a hit. Three in a row is a critical hit. Monsters come back 30 seconds after they fall.
    • Monsters are as strong as their floor: two wrong answers against a monster of your level and you faint. Fainting costs XP, and fainting again and again can drop your level.
    • Classmates who answer at least one question in your battle share the XP, so fighting alone earns more.
    • Open chests to find hint scrolls and healing potions. In a battle, a hint removes two wrong answers. A potion heals half your health. Some chests on higher floors bite back!
    • A wrong answer lets the monster attack, and shows you the rule. Read it, it helps next time.
    • Classmates can fight the same monster. Every hit counts for the team.
    • Crimson is an assassin. His critical hits strike for 3 instead of 2.
    • Sweetlillac is a healer. Every third correct answer in a row blooms flowers that heal her and every classmate fighting the same monster.
    • The staircase is guarded. Beat the guardian, then step on the stairs. Everyone must be ready before the class climbs.
    • Floors 1–10 are the dungeon, 11–20 the cellars, 21–30 the castle, 31–40 the tower. Something waits on floor 41: a level 100 boss that the whole class must fight together.
